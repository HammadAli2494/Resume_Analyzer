Senior Data Scientist

About the role
We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to own predictive modeling, experimentation,
and analytics for our B2B SaaS platform. You will work with product, engineering, and
go-to-market teams to turn data into decisions.

Requirements
• 3+ years of experience in data science or applied machine learning roles
• Strong Python skills (pandas, scikit-learn) and production-quality code
• Advanced SQL and experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred)
• Solid foundation in statistics, hypothesis testing, and A/B experimentation
• Experience building and evaluating supervised learning models (classification, regression)
• Ability to explain technical results to non-technical stakeholders
• Familiarity with cloud deployment (AWS) and containerization (Docker) is a plus

Nice to have
• M.S. or Ph.D. in Statistics, Computer Science, or a quantitative field
• Experience with churn, LTV, or customer analytics in SaaS
• Tableau or similar BI tooling

Responsibilities
• Develop and maintain ML models for customer success and revenue teams
• Design and analyze product experiments
• Collaborate on data pipelines and metric definitions with data engineering
